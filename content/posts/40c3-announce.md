This year again we’re getting ready for c3stoc at 40c3!

And we’re going to stick to the proven process from the past years as well.
Our goal is again to make sure there are sticker boxes spread across the event, with fresh stickers regularly delivered to them.
Thank you to our friends at [Chaos Post](https://chaospost.de/) for making this possible again! 

## Summary of how the Sticker Operation Center works

We are going to plan sticker boxes (exchange points) all across Messe Hamburg.
If you would like to run a sticker box at your assembly, read the [guidelines](/40c3-c3stoc-stickerbox-guidelines.pdf) first and please send us a private message on [Mastodon](https://chaos.social/@c3stoc) or [send us an email](mailto:exchange@stickeroperation.center).
We will announce the locations of the sticker exchange points shortly before Congress starts.
You will then be able to either drop stickers directly into the boxes or bring them to Chaos Post to have them delivered to the exchange points.

In the meantime, we look forward to seeing pictures of your designs on [Mastodon](https://chaos.social/@c3stoc)—tag us and we’ll share them!
If you’re still working on your designs, take a look at our [guide](/how-to-sticker/).

## And again, a short FAQ

### I want my assembly to have a sticker box. What should I do?

That’s great!
Please let us know as soon as possible, either [by email](mailto:exchange@stickeroperation.center) or via a [private message on chaos.social](https://chaos.social/@c3stoc). 
We will get back to you as soon as we’ve decided where to put the boxes.
If we receive more requests than we can handle, we will have to select which assemblies receive a box.

We have  [guidelines for assemblies who want to run a sticker box](/40c3-c3stoc-stickerbox-guidelines.pdf) – please make sure you read them carefully!
You will have to explicitly agree to the rules, as we had some issues in the past.

**⚠️ Make sure to let us know before December 20th, 18:00 Hamburg time if you want to host a sticker exchange!**

Of course, if we don’t select your box, it doesn’t mean that your assembly shouldn’t have a sticker exchange.
The more, the better!
It just means that we won’t be distributing stickers to it, as we can’t handle too many boxes.
However, experience has shown that people will find it anyways and fill it by themselves, if it just exists!

### I want to create some stickers this year. Can you help?

We cannot design or print them for you, but we’re happy to help where we can.
To avoid repeating ourselves, we created the [sticker making guide](/how-to-sticker/) mentioned in our post above.
Have a look at it; it should answer most of your questions.
If not, you can contact us on [Mastodon](https://chaos.social/@c3stoc) or [via email](mailto:exchange@stickeroperation.center).
We’re also happy to take a look at your design if you’re not sure about it, so feel free to send us a picture of what you’d like to print. 

### I have ideas, I like stickers and I have time. Can I help?

We already have a small team for this year and currently don’t think we need more people.
But it’s good to know about potential helping hands, so feel free to get in touch anyways!
Please don’t be sad if we won’t contact you for help though.
That just means there’s more time for finding stickers!

If you have any other ideas, we can discuss them and work out together whether they are realistically doable without causing additional work or creating issues.
Even if it doesn’t work out this year, we’d still love to hear what other sticker enthusiasts want to do.

Also, we recommend supporting Chaos Post as a Chaos Carrier.
They’re awesome and they’re playing a central role in the c3stoc infrastructure: help them deliver stickers to the boxes, or if there are none to deliver at the time, deliver some postcards instead!
It’s more fun than one would think, trust me ;)
You can check out their website at [chaospost.de](https://chaospost.de/).

There won’t be any volunteer shifts in the Engelsystem related to the Sticker Operation Center.

### I am not attending Congress. Can you mail me stickers as you used to?

Unfortunately not.
We only did that when there was no in-person event.
We understand that not everyone can attend and we would love to help, but unfortunately we don’t have the capacity to deal with that on top of our usual in-person event activities.
The remote exchanges were very time-consuming and stressful, and we’re quite relieved to be back to our initial mode.

### I won’t be attending Congress or I will be there but won’t be able to carry my stickers with me. Can I send you some stickers to distribute on my behalf?

Unfortunately, we can’t offer that service.
We are travelling to Congress by train and are struggling to bring large amounts of our own stickers already.
